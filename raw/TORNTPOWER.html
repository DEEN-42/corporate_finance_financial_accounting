About

Torrent Power Ltd is a leading integrated power utility company in India with presence across generation, transmission and distribution of power. Its operations are spread across the states of Gujarat, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka.[1]

It is a part of Torrent Group which has presence across pharmaceutical, power and city gas distribution business.[2]

Key Points

Business Segments:[1]
a) Transmission:
Operational network of 952 ckm at 400 kV
and 388 ckm at 220 kV; ~40 kms of additional 400 kV D/C line in the pipeline.
b) Distribution:
Stable, regulated revenue stream through licensed distribution in Ahmedabad, Surat, Dahej SEZ, Dholera SIR, DNH, and Diu & Daman; plus franchised operations in Agra, Bhiwandi, Shil, Mumbra & Kalwa.
c) Thermal Generation:
The backbone of the portfolio with 2,730 MW gas-based and 362 MW coal-based capacity operational. An additional 1,600 MW coal capacity is under development, and 1,400 MW is under acquisition, indicating aggressive capacity expansion.
d) Renewable Generation:
A fast-growing pillar. Solar stands at 1,081 MWp operational with 1,787 MWp in the pipeline. Wind is 921 MW operational with a large 2,163 MW under development - total renewable pipeline exceeds 3,900 MW, signaling a strong green transition.
e) PSP (Pumped Storage Projects):
An emerging high-growth vertical with
8.4 GW pipeline across UP and Maharashtra;
3 GW under construction and 2 GW tied up - strategically important for grid balancing as renewables scale.
f) Green Hydrogen / Green Ammonia:
Early-stage but PLI-backed; awarded 18 KTPA capacity under the government's PLI Scheme, positioning Torrent for the emerging clean fuel economy.
